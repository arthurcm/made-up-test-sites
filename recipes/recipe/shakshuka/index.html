Shakshuka for two

♡

Ready in 25 minutes. Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce.

You need

4 eggs, 1 tin of tomatoes, 1 red pepper, 1 onion, cumin, paprika.

Method

Soften the onion and pepper for 8 minutes, add the spices and tomatoes, simmer 10 minutes, then poach the eggs in the sauce for 6 minutes.

Tested in our kitchen on 14 November 2026.
